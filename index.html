About Me

I am a cloud infrastructure engineer with a deep-seated passion for automating the entire lifecycle of distributed systems — from zero-touch provisioning to self-healing deployments. My focus is on engineering environments that sustain 99.9% high availability, accelerate deployment frequency through GitOps workflows, and eliminate toil through intelligent automation across AWS, Azure, and hybrid Kubernetes landscapes.

My technical foundation is anchored by a Master of Science in Information Systems from California State University, Long Beach, where I deepened my expertise in systems analysis, database management, and applied machine learning. This builds upon a Bachelor of Engineering in Information Science from Visvesvaraya Technological University, giving me a rigorous grounding in computer architecture, networking fundamentals, and operating systems — the bedrock of modern cloud engineering.

Professionally, I bridge two distinct engineering worlds: the high-stakes enterprise fintech environment at Fidelity National Information Services (FIS), where I managed 200+ servers across 25+ client environments and drove critical automation initiatives at scale, and the agile, mission-driven cloud landscape at Saayam For All, where I architected AWS core infrastructure from scratch using Infrastructure-as-Code principles. This dual exposure — enterprise resilience meets startup velocity — shapes my approach to every system I build.

My engineering philosophy centers on designing infrastructure that is invisible in success and transparent in failure. I build scalable microservices architectures with self-healing Kubernetes configurations, implement robust CI/CD pipelines that enable daily deployments with confidence, and embed observability at every layer so teams can act on signals, not noise. I believe the best infrastructure is the kind your application never has to think about.
