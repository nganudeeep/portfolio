About Me

I am a DevOps and Site Reliability Engineer with 3 years of full-time experience in enterprise fintech, plus hands-on AWS volunteer work in the non-profit space. My focus is automating the entire lifecycle of distributed systems — from zero-touch provisioning to self-healing deployments — while maintaining 99.9% uptime across large server fleets. I am comfortable being the first responder when something breaks, with a strong track record in Linux troubleshooting, incident response, and root-cause analysis.

My technical foundation is anchored by a Master of Science in Information Systems from California State University, Long Beach (GPA 3.5), building on a Bachelor of Engineering in Information Science from Visvesvaraya Technological University (GPA 3.7). Coursework in systems analysis, database management, machine learning, data structures, and networking gives me depth across both the infrastructure and software layers of modern cloud systems.

Professionally, I bridge two engineering environments: the high-stakes enterprise fintech world at FIS, where I managed 200+ servers across 25+ client environments, built Terraform/Ansible automation, operated Kubernetes clusters, and drove DevSecOps practices at scale — and agile cloud-native work at Saayam For All, where I architected AWS infrastructure from scratch using IaC, set up CloudWatch observability, and built multi-branch CI/CD pipelines.

My engineering philosophy is straightforward: security and observability are not afterthoughts. I integrate Trivy container scanning and SonarQube static analysis into pipelines, instrument systems with Prometheus and Grafana dashboards before they go to production, and build GitOps workflows with Argo CD so deployments are auditable, repeatable, and rollback-ready. The best infrastructure is the kind your on-call engineer never has to page about at 3 AM.
